Services

Tax and bookkeeping services built for individuals and small businesses.

Every engagement starts with a real conversation about your situation — not a one-size-fits-all checklist.

Individuals

Individual Tax Preparation

Federal and state return preparation for individuals and families, including more complex situations: multiple income sources, investment income, self-employment, and life changes like a new home, marriage, or a growing family.

  • Federal & state return preparation and e-filing
  • Prior-year and amended returns
  • Self-employed & 1099 income
  • Year-round questions — not just during filing season

Good fit if you...

Want a preparer who explains your return instead of just filing it, or your situation has gotten too complex for DIY software.

Good fit if you...

Run a small business or side business and want your bookkeeping and tax return to actually be consistent with each other.

Small Business

Business Tax Preparation

Tax preparation for sole proprietors, partnerships, and small corporations — S-Corps, C-Corps, LLCs — filed with the care your finances deserve.

  • Business tax return preparation (Schedule C, 1120, 1120-S, 1065)
  • Quarterly estimated tax calculations
  • Entity structure & deduction guidance
Bookkeeping

Books That Don't Wait Until April to Make Sense

QuickBooks-based bookkeeping for small businesses that want accurate records all year — not a scramble the week before filing. When your books and your tax return come from the same desk, both get easier.

  • Monthly reconciliation — bank and credit card accounts kept current, not caught up
  • QuickBooks setup & cleanup — for businesses starting fresh or fixing a mess
  • Categorized, tax-ready records — so filing season isn't a surprise
  • Monthly financial snapshots — a clear read on how the business is actually doing

Good fit if you...

Are behind on reconciling accounts, dread opening QuickBooks, or just want one person handling books and taxes.
