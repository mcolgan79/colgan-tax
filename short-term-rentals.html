Short-Term Rental Tax

Tax help built specifically for short-term rental owners.

Airbnb, VRBO, and vacation rental income comes with rules a general preparer can easily miss — especially in Florida. This is what I focus on.

Schedule a Consultation
Why This Is a Specialty

Short-term rentals aren't taxed like a normal rental property.

⚖

Schedule C vs. Schedule E

Whether your rental is treated as active business income or passive rental income changes your tax bill and your self-employment tax exposure — getting this wrong is common and costly.

🏚

Florida-Specific Rules

Florida sales tax and county tourist development (bed) taxes apply to most short-term stays under 6 months — separate from federal income tax, and easy to overlook.

📈

Depreciation & Cost Segregation

Furnished rental properties open up depreciation strategies, including cost segregation, that can meaningfully reduce taxable income when done correctly.

📋

Material Participation

The "material participation" tests determine whether losses can offset other income — a common area where self-filed returns miss out on legitimate deductions.

🏦

Multiple Properties & Entities

Guidance on whether to hold rentals personally, in an LLC, or across multiple entities as your portfolio grows.

💳

Platform 1099s Reconciliation

Reconciling Airbnb, VRBO, and booking platform 1099-K/1099-MISC forms against your actual bookkeeping so nothing gets double-counted or missed.

Who This Is For

Built for owners with one property or a growing portfolio.

  • First-time short-term rental owners who just started hosting
  • Owners with multiple properties across Florida counties
  • Investors weighing whether to convert a long-term rental to short-term
  • Anyone who got a surprise tax bill last year and wants it fixed going forward

What you'll walk away with

A return prepared with STR rules applied correctly, plus a clear plan for next year — estimated payments, recordkeeping, and any local tax registrations you need in place.
